# QSol Analytics Website Product/Service PDR + PRD Pack

## Purpose of this document
This document defines how QSol Analytics should present its current service toolbox on the website.

It is designed to be passed directly to website, copy, and implementation agents.

The goal is not to rebuild the brand architecture from scratch. The current site already has a usable high-level structure. Instead, this document expands the current service offering into 8 concrete, understandable, commercially credible offers.

These 8 offers should be treated as the current toolbox that QSol Analytics can realistically provide now.

---

# 1. Website architecture decision

## Recommended structure
Keep the current 4 top-level service categories as the main navigation structure:

1. Operational Data Systems
2. Predictive Analytics & Forecasting
3. Machine Learning Solutions
4. AI & Intelligent Automation

Under these, introduce the 8 concrete products/services as the actual solution-level offers.

## Why this structure should be kept
- The current top-level structure is already clean and easy to scan.
- Four categories are easier for users to navigate than eight equal-weight service pages in the nav.
- The 8-offer toolbox is best used to increase clarity, not to create menu clutter.
- This allows service pages to rank around broader themes, while the offer blocks and subpages capture more specific buying intent.

## Mapping of the 8 offers into the current website structure

### Operational Data Systems
- Reporting and Workflow Automation for SMEs
- Decision-Support Tools and Small Internal Apps
- Data Audits and AI Readiness Audits

### Predictive Analytics & Forecasting
- Forecasting Services
- Anomaly Detection and Monitoring

### Machine Learning Solutions
- Customer Segmentation and Commercial Insight

### AI & Intelligent Automation
- Lightweight NLP Solutions
- Internal AI Assistants Grounded on Company Documents

## Website implementation principle
The site should speak in terms of business problems, deliverables, timelines, and outcomes.
Do not make the site read like a technical capability inventory.
The technical depth should support credibility, not lead the message.

---

# 2. Global implementation rules for agents

## Messaging rules
1. Lead with business pain and business outcome.
2. Avoid jargon unless it is immediately translated.
3. Do not oversell “AI”.
4. Frame systems as practical, narrow, auditable, and useful.
5. Use language that feels credible to SME operators, not startup hype language.
6. Keep the tone calm, concrete, and commercially literate.
7. Where possible, describe what the client receives, not only what QSol does.

## Copy pattern for every service block/page
Each offer should use this sequence:
1. What it is
2. Who it is for
3. Problems it solves
4. What is delivered
5. Why it creates value
6. Typical example use cases
7. What is needed from the client
8. Typical timeline
9. CTA

## Design rules
1. Keep pages highly scannable.
2. Use section headers that answer buyer questions.
3. Use bullets where they increase clarity.
4. Add “Typical outputs” and “Typical results” sections.
5. Add internal links between related offers.
6. Reuse case studies as proof under relevant offers.

## CTA rules
Use soft but concrete CTAs such as:
- Book a discovery call
- Discuss your workflow
- See where automation would save time
- Explore whether this fits your data

Avoid vague CTAs like:
- Transform your future
- Unlock AI-powered growth today

# 3. Product/Service PDR + PRD: Reporting and Workflow Automation for SMEs

## Product name
Reporting and Workflow Automation

## Category
Operational Data Systems

## Positioning summary
Automation for spreadsheet-heavy teams who are losing time to repetitive reporting, copying data between systems, and manual data preparation.

## Website purpose
Show this as one of the most immediate, practical, and high-ROI services QSol offers.

## User problem
The client has recurring operational or reporting work that is manually repeated every week or month.
This usually includes spreadsheet merging, copying between systems, formatting reports, checking missing data, or sending updates.

## Business pains to address on site
- Reports take hours to produce
- Important data lives in multiple places
- Manual copy-paste introduces errors
- Teams cannot see issues until too late
- Headcount pressure rises because processes do not scale

## What the service is
A service that automates data ingestion, cleaning, validation, merging, reporting, and alerting so recurring work happens reliably with less manual effort.

## What can be provided
- Automated Excel, CSV, API, or database ingestion
- Data cleaning and transformation pipelines
- Validation checks and error flags
- Scheduled KPI reports
- Dashboard feeds
- Threshold alerts and missing-data alerts
- Semi-automated workflows with human review where needed

## Tangible deliverables
- Workflow map of current vs improved process
- Automated reporting pipeline
- Rules for data validation
- Dashboard or output report template
- Documentation and handover notes
- Optional support and iteration plan

## Business value description
This service reduces repetitive admin load, lowers error rates, improves reporting consistency, and gives teams faster visibility into what is happening.
Instead of paying skilled staff to repeatedly assemble information, the business gets a repeatable system that produces the same output with less delay and less fragility.

## Website wording: clear version
### What it is
We automate the recurring reporting and data-handling work that slows teams down.

### Simple explanation
If your team is spending hours every week pulling numbers from spreadsheets, cleaning data, combining files, and building the same reports again and again, we can turn that into a repeatable system.

### Value wording
This saves time, reduces manual errors, and gives you faster visibility without adding headcount.

## Example use cases
- Weekly operations report built automatically from multiple spreadsheets
- Site-visit data cleaned and turned into a standard reporting pack
- Finance and operations files merged into one KPI summary
- Missing entries flagged before they affect downstream reporting

## Best-fit clients
- Operations-heavy SMEs
- Spreadsheet-led teams
- Businesses with recurring weekly/monthly reporting
- Firms using several disconnected tools

## Inputs required from client
- Sample files or current reports
- Overview of current process
- Business rules and KPI definitions
- Access to relevant systems or exports

## Typical timeline
2 to 4 weeks for focused projects

## Proof to connect
- Acoustic consultancy automation case study
- Estimating / MIS case study where relevant

## CTA copy
Book a call to identify the reporting work that should already be automated.

## Agent implementation instructions
- Add this offer as a solution card under Operational Data Systems.
- Add a subsection on the Operational Data Systems page titled “Reporting and Workflow Automation”.
- Include a “What you get” block and a “Typical examples” block.
- Link to relevant case studies.
- Ensure homepage references this as the lead practical service.
